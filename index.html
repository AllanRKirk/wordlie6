How to Play Wordlie6

Guess the 6‑letter word in six tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid English word.
  • Green: correct letter, correct spot.
  • Gold: correct letter, wrong spot.
  • Gray: letter not in the word.
  • Use the on-screen or physical keyboard to enter letters.
  • Press ENTER to submit, DELETE to erase.
  • “Show Hint” gives one random letter.

Solve as many words as you can and keep your streak going!
